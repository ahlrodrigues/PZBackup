description=[b]PZ Backup[/b] copies your save [b]while you play[/b] — every 5 minutes and whenever you hit [b]F8[/b]. Died? It restores your latest backup, reopens the game, and you just click [b]Continue[/b].

description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Auto backup[/b] every 5 min while you play, plus one when the game closes
description=[*][b]F8[/b] = backup right now. [b]F9[/b] = restore window (keys configurable)
description=[*][b]Died? Undo![/b] 10 s countdown → the game closes, restores the latest backup of [i]that world[/i], reopens → click [b]Continue[/b]
description=[*][b]Restore now / Choose another / Cancel[/b] — your call
description=[*][b]Never backs up a corpse[/b]: no backups while you're dead, so the good ones stay safe
description=[*][b]Auto cleanup[/b] of dead-character saves from the Load list (kept in a safety folder)
description=[*][b]View log[/b] button, color-coded, right in the game
description=[*]Speaks [b]English and Portuguese[/b] (follows your game language)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahlrodrigues/PZBackup/main/docs/img/morreu-en.png[/img]
description=
description=[h2]Tips[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Bites are forever[/b]: if the latest backup was taken after a bite, use [i]Choose another[/i].
description=[*]After a restore, check the save's mods ([i]Load → save → Mods[/i]) and re-enable PZ Backup if needed.
description=[*]Want hardcore? Turn auto-restore off in [i]Options → Mods → PZ Backup[/i].
description=[/list]
